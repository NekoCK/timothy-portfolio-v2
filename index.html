My Process

Clarify first, decide next, then make the decision stick.

I move ambiguous product work forward through three deliberate steps.

Surface symptoms → real constraintsCompare options → make trade-offsOne solution → a system the team can reuse
01

Make the problem clear

Turn scattered requests, symptoms, and constraints into the core problem the team actually needs to solve.

02

Make the direction decidable

Make options, risks, and trade-offs visible so the team can move from discussion to an actionable product decision.

03

Make the decision sustainable

Turn one-off solutions into reusable product rules, workflows, or systems that reduce rework and coordination.

What teams get

Turn clear judgment into a foundation the team can move forward with.

Reduce false starts

Clarify the real problem, constraints, and trade-offs before development starts, so the team is less likely to restart halfway through.

Reduce alignment loops

Give product, design, engineering, and business teams the same problem and options, so they can decide the next step faster.

Stop starting from zero

Turn effective approaches into reusable rules and foundations so future delivery does not need to rediscover the same answers.
